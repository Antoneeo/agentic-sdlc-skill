# Client Dossier — Caffè Brancaleone (synthetic, ground truth)

Fictional but realistic Italian SMB. This file is the ONLY source of truth the
client-subagent may draw facts from. The eval agent (skill executor) must NOT
read this file — it learns facts only through elicitation.

## Business

- **Caffè Brancaleone** — torrefazione artigianale, Bergamo. Founded 2018 by
  Marco Brancaleone (45, ex barista di terza generazione).
- Products: specialty coffee, small-batch roasted. Blends + single origin.
  Whole bean and ground; 250g/1kg formats. B2C subscriptions NOT offered today.
- Revenue mix: B2B bars/restaurants ~60%, physical shop at the roastery ~25%,
  e-commerce (Shopify) ~15%.
- Annual revenue: ~€380k. Gross margin ~55% B2C, ~35% B2B.
- Pricing: B2C €28-35/kg (a 250g bag sells at €8.50-9.50); B2B €18-22/kg.

## Channels & assets

- **B2B**: 42 active bar/restaurant clients between Bergamo and Brescia. Low
  churn (2-3 lost/year), flat for 2 years. Marco sells in person; no
  salespeople. Typical B2B cycle: 2-3 visits over a month, free tasting.
- **E-commerce**: ~90 orders/month, AOV €38, ~3,000 visits/month, mostly
  branded/local organic + word of mouth. One DIY Facebook ads attempt in 2023:
  €800 spent, "buttati via" (no tracking was set up).
- **Newsletter**: 1,200 subscribers (shop + online customers), sporadic sends
  (last one 3 months ago). Mailchimp free tier.
- **Instagram**: 2,800 followers, irregular posting by niece Giulia.
- **Physical shop**: works well, loyal local base, hosts occasional cupping
  events (~20 people, 4x/year).
- Website: Shopify, decent product pages, no blog, Italian only.

## Team & capacity

- Marco (owner): production + B2B sales + everything else. Can dedicate ~4h/week
  to marketing tasks, reluctantly.
- 2 production employees (no marketing role).
- Giulia (niece, 24, part-time): 8h/week for social + site. Decent with
  Instagram/Canva, never run paid ads.
- No agency. Bad past experience: a local web agency (2022) charged €4,800 for
  a "digital strategy" PDF nobody used.

## Money & goals

- Stated budget: "massimo 1.000-1.500 euro al mese, e voglio vedere risultati".
  Can stretch to €2,000/month if something demonstrably works.
- Initial stated goal (vague): "vendere di più online; il negozio e i bar
  vanno bene ma l'online è fermo da due anni".
- If pushed: would like e-commerce to reach 25% of revenue within a year.
- Seasonality: Nov-Dec peak (gifts, +60% online), summer dip.

## Constraints & attitudes

- NO TikTok ("non è roba per noi").
- Hates aggressive discounting; max acceptable: welcome 10% on first order.
- Artisan image is sacred: no stock photos, no "roba da multinazionale".
- Diffident about marketing after the 2022 agency burn; wants plain talk.
- Perceived competitors (seeds): "i grossi tipo Illy/Lavazza non li conto; i
  veri concorrenti sono le torrefazioni specialty che vendono online — His
  Majesty the Coffee, Ditta Artigianale, quelli lì".
- Target language/market: Italian, Italy (would not say no to export "un
  domani", but not now).

## Facts Marco does NOT know (must answer "non lo so" if asked)

- Precise e-commerce conversion rate, CAC, LTV, email open rates.
- Competitor pricing details beyond "mi pare costino come noi o poco più".
- Any industry benchmark.
