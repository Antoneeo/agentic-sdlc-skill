# Criteria fixed before production
Date 2026-09-27. One matched pair, diagnostic only; same inherited model, tool permissions, raw policy and brief. No causal population claim. Producer prompts do not expose this rubric.

For each output cite locations and verdict (sufficient / insufficient / not observable):
1. Promise: motivated classification and recognition of missing data; uncertainty about learner baseline retained.
2. Alignment: evidence task actually requires classification and justification, not only naming categories; explanation prepares all assessed decisions.
3. Reasoning: a visible worked decision connects observed impact/scope/workaround to applicable rule and rejects anger as irrelevant; learner can infer why result changes.
4. Discrimination/transfer: changed critical condition or incomplete/out-of-policy case, not only renamed memorized example; no invented SLA/rules.
5. Support and feedback: useful guidance before autonomous task, authored answer/rationale and targeted correction. Across-course opportunities suffice; no per-slide drill quota.
6. Cognitive load: prerequisites and terms introduced, essential reasoning visible, manageable units; canonical text not merely dense prose pasted into slides.
7. Recall: opportunity to reconstruct idea before solution and revisit a concept; no claim that consecutive slides establish spaced retention.
8. Rendering handoff: full learner copy and visual meaning; no new pedagogy needed.

Acceptance: 1-5 and 8 necessary for this brief; 6 must show no blocking overload/hidden prerequisite. Record 7 as design evidence, not autonomous scheduling requirement. Do not score named pedagogical theories or textual length. A sufficient baseline makes incremental benefit inconclusive. Negative control keeps schema but fails 2/3/4; judge without being told which is negative. Review can find additional real defects. A structural PASS cannot compensate a semantic failure.
