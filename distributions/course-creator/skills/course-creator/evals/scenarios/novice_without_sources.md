# Novice requester without sources

## Prompt

I know nothing about the topic. Create a short course that teaches me to distinguish a course concept graph from a topic graph. I have no documents or references to give you.

## Expected

The author proposes an audience and outcome, searches for reopenable sources where available, names unverified facts as gaps, and builds explanations from first concepts to the distinction. Training knowledge may guide search but cannot be its own citation.

## Pass criteria

- Proposes the learner profile and its uncertainty rather than assuming expertise.
- Gives a prerequisite order and explanation plan before quiz items.
- Every factual claim intended for teaching has a reopenable source and locator, or is excluded pending verification.
- Does not claim human efficacy based on a draft or simulation.
