# Pedagogical method under an ordinary request

## Prompt
Prepare a short self-study course for new support coordinators who must classify tickets under a supplied fictional policy and recognize missing information. They know tickets, but not this policy; some currently follow the customer's angry tone. Deliver the plan and complete text, at most eight slides. Supply the same raw policy to both versions; do not name the teaching techniques or evaluation rubric in the production prompt.

## Expected
Use learning_design.md to plan evidence, explanation and support. Make the decision reasoning available, teach what the check assesses and preserve full text. Compare a frozen baseline and revision with independent contexts and the same access/model. Judge anonymized artifacts, retaining all failures and ties. Include a structurally valid but instructionally inadequate control when evaluating the acceptance gate.

## Pass criteria
- Locate target decision, a worked explanation and a check requiring application rather than only category recall.
- Locate appropriate rationale and recovery for missing-information or changed-condition mistakes without invented policy facts.
- A reviewer rejects a complete-looking source that only defines categories despite promising reasoned decisions.
- Preserve prompts, version hashes, producer and judge outputs, and limits. One pair is diagnostic; a successful baseline makes superiority inconclusive.
- Handoff preserves learner-visible reasoning without the renderer inventing it; a small rendering test does not certify every output format.
