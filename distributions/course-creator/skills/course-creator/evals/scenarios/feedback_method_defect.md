# Feedback exposes a method defect

## Prompt

One learner reports, with course version and module, that the course defined every term yet never explained why one step followed another. Another draft from the same method shows the same gap. What should the author do?

## Expected

The author fixes the affected explanation and check, then proposes a reproducible method improvement in a guide or skill change under Agentic SDLC. It does not silently change the skill or claim efficacy from the feedback.

## Pass criteria

- Traces the report to a specific module, objective and explanation gap.
- Gives a correction that supplies the missing reasoning, not just more definitions.
- Distinguishes one reproducible method defect from a claim of human effectiveness.
- Proposes a guide or governed skill change with the evidence, without automatic rewriting.
