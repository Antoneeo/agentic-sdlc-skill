# Marketing Vision
Status: APPROVED (by the owner, 2026-01-01)

## Business Goal
Reach 24 paying customers in six months on the budget the owner actually has [EV-01],
without buying reach the account cannot sustain afterwards [EV-03].

## Positioning
The specialist option for teams that already know what they need. Not the cheapest,
and not trying to be.

## Non-Goals
- No discount-led acquisition: the competitor's pre-launch discount window [EV-06] is
  something to survive, not to match.
- No channel the owner cannot run alone.
