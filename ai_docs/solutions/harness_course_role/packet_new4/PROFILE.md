# Learner profile (from the course D-UC)

P1 — a person on a software team who works with AI agents (Claude or Codex). The common path serves both the developer, who must steer a concrete change, and the technical lead, who must judge benefit, risk and decisions. Ordinary agent use is known; knowledge of the two skills (kb-agentic, agentic-sdlc) is not established.

The course is about working with an agent that uses the kb-agentic and agentic-sdlc skills. Self-study delivery.
