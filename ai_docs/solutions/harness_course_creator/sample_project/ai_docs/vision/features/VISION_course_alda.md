# Course Vision: Alda receipts
Status: APPROVED

This is a synthetic approved-state fixture for validator and simulation tests; no human approved a real Alda course.

## Expected Benefit
A novice can decide whether a fictional Alda receipt is valid by applying its declared rules.
## Learners and Starting Point
A reader who can add small whole numbers, knows none of Alda's invented terms, and will inspect receipts on paper.
## Promised Understanding and Ability
Read token values, rotate the first token, compute the check total, and compare it to the seal.
## Success Signals
On a new receipt, identify the rotated token, compute the total, and explain the verdict.
## Scope and Non-Goals
This is a fictional protocol used to test course creation; it makes no claim about a real standard or human learning.
